# FUTURE_DS_01

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 1 (2026)

### Business Sales Performance Analytics

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1 (2026)**.

The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data and transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Python, Jupyter Notebook, Excel, and Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on revenue trends, product performance, category performance, regional performance, and overall business KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Clean and prepare the sales dataset
- Analyze revenue trends over time
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze category performance
- Analyze regional performance
- Compare revenue and profit
- Identify important business insights
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide actionable business recommendations

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Products

Products generating strong revenue and profit should receive more marketing and promotional attention.

### 2. Improve Low-Profit Products

Products with high sales but low profitability should be reviewed for pricing, discounts, costs and margins.

### 3. Strengthen High-Performing Regions

Regions with strong revenue and profitability can be prioritized for further growth and expansion.

### 4. Monitor Category Performance

Category performance should be reviewed regularly to identify growth opportunities and weaker categories.

### 5. Use Data-Driven Decision Making

The Power BI dashboard can be used to monitor business performance and support better strategic decisions.
